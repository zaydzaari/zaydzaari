## Research

### [Forest-loss detection across geographic regions](https://github.com/zaydzaari/geographic-training-diversity-forest-loss)

A fixed-budget study comparing RGB and multispectral satellite imagery for forest-loss detection in the Brazilian Amazon.

- **107 configurations · 642 trained models · 2,568 evaluations**
- Multispectral models performed better at every tested region count. The study found no clear evidence that the size of that advantage changed systematically with the number of training regions.
- Code, experiment configurations, frozen results, and reproducibility materials are available. **Manuscript in preparation; not yet published.**

[Research repository](https://github.com/zaydzaari/geographic-training-diversity-forest-loss) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0007-5967-1692)
